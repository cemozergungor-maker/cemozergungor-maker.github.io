Harbor Sort – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Harbor Sort ("the game"), published by Cem Ozer Gungor, handles information. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (level, stars, coins, harbor buildings, settings and purchases you made) is saved on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes the local copy (see Cloud save below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time and performance),
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game stores a copy of its save file (the same progress data listed above) in your Google account through Google Play Games Services, so your progress can be restored after reinstalling or on a new device. The game does not see your password or contacts. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete the saved game in your Google account's Play Games settings.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase was made so it can deliver the item.
